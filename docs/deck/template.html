The solution read threshold

Four things, built and running on your grid today.

A registry that measures what each camera can resolve Plate width in pixels, clock drift, delivered frame rate, night usability — measured from the camera's own video, never read off a datasheet. cameras carry it now.
A control plane that assigns analytics by capability Plate reading is scheduled only where a plate is legible. On this grid that is government cameras in . The rest do cheap presence work and corroborate.
An officer's console that shows its working Search a registration, get a route with a confidence and a reason for every sighting, the vehicle and the plate crop the reader worked from, and a court-ready bundle in one action.
Governance enforced in code, not in a policy annex Case-linked access refused before a row is read, a tamper-evident audit chain over every query and export, and a confidence tier capped by what the camera was measured able to do.

Governance as architecture read threshold

A bundle that survives being challenged by someone who does not trust me.

Refused before a row is read A movement query names the case, the searching officer and the officer authorising it. Without all three it is refused — and the refusal is logged.
Tamper-evident by constructionEvery entry hashes the one before it. Editing or deleting one breaks the chain and names the row that broke. entries verify today.
The bundle hashes itself into that chainSHA-256 over the exported object, written to the log as it leaves. The camera block carries measured capability, so "how do you know it was that vehicle" has a number for an answer.
Evidence, not export
Faces, persons and safety face floor

Facial recognition is assigned by the same control plane — and on this grid it assigns it to nothing.

Face matching is limited by inter-ocular distance. ISO/IEC 19794-5 puts 60px as the enrolment floor; I use a generous px to even attempt one, derived from person detections through published anthropometry so a reviewer can recompute it. The console carries it per camera, beside plate width — the same question, asked of people.
best camera measured Short by a factor of . The worst gives px — not a face, a smudge that resembles one.
cameras assigned face matching Measured across people on cameras. Onboard one that clears the floor and it is assigned automatically, capped at probable.
3 layers person safety without a weapon detector A knife is a 2px smear here. Twenty people running from one point is a third of the frame — that is what is detected. False alarms measured on real government footage; detection measured on simulation.
Tracking, yes. Biometric identification, not on this grid.

What is proven, and what is not read threshold

Underclaimed on purpose. The next stage is live feeds in front of you.

Running today
Measured on the real grid of government cameras surveyed, failures, capability and clock drift recorded per camera.
End to end on live feeds Ingest forced over TCP, timing from PTS, reconnect with backoff — demonstrated on government cameras and on the entrant's own footage.
Tested automated tests pass, including the organisers' own pre-submission checklist as executable acceptance tests.
Designed, not yet built
Authentication and RBACThe case-linked gate is real and tested; the identity behind it belongs to eGujCop or departmental AD with MFA. Stated plainly rather than implied.
Masking on export Uninvolved parties should be obscured in bundles. The hosted build withholds them; obscuring them inside an image a case is entitled to is not built.
Person re-identification and dispersalValidated in simulation only. There is no government footage of an attack, and there will not be one.
Next: the live test on 12–13 October — ~50 heterogeneous cameras onboarded, a designated vehicle tracked, alerts, movement history and GIS, on the infrastructure the state already owns.
