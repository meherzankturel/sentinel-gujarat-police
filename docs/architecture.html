Sentinel — how the system works

Integrated Video Management & Analytics Platform · Gujarat Police Hackathon Innovation Challenge 2026 · Meherzan Turel, individual entrant

1

The cameras

30 government cameras in the test grid. Around 80,000 across Gujarat, owned by 26 different departments. Different makes, different ages, and none of them were built to work together.

2

Connect to each camera

Open a camera, keep the picture steady, and cope when the feed drops — reconnect on its own, wait a little longer each time, and never lose track of when each frame was actually filmed.

3

Find out what each camera can really see

Watch each camera for a minute and measure it. How big do vehicles actually look? Does the camera swivel? How dark does it get at night? Is its clock telling the truth?

This is the important part. Nothing can watch 80,000 cameras at once — so each camera is given only the jobs it is physically able to do. A camera that can't read a number plate isn't asked to try.

4

Is this worth an officer's time?

Too many alerts is what makes these systems fail — officers stop reading them. So every sighting is graded, and only the strong ones interrupt anyone.

Sure
Good camera, clear reading. Raise the alert.
Fairly sure
Raise it, but show the officer why it is not certain.
Not sure
Don't interrupt anyone. Quietly add it to the evidence.
5

What the officer sees

One screen. Search for a vehicle, see everywhere it has been, and hand the file to a court.

Map of every camera and what it can do The vehicle's route, with a confidence on each sighting Where it is likely to appear next Court-ready evidence file in one click

Built in from the start

Every search is logged
Each one records the case it belongs to and the officer who authorised it.
The log can't be edited quietly
Each entry is sealed against the one before it, so any change shows up.
Bystanders masked on export
Designed, not yet built, and named here rather than implied. Today an uninvolved person's record is withheld entirely; obscuring them inside an image a case is entitled to is the remaining work.
No face recognition
A deliberate choice. The system detects what people do, not who they are.
